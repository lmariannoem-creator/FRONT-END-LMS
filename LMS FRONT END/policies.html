The Clare Library System — Policies and Guidelines

1. User Account Policy

  • Every user must register or log in using valid account credentials.
  • Each user must provide accurate information during registration.
  • Users must keep their login credentials confidential.
  • Users must not access another person's account without permission.
  • The administrator has the authority to manage user accounts and permissions.

2. Book Borrowing Policy

  • Users must have an active library account to borrow books.
  • Each user may borrow a maximum of three books at a time.
  • Books must be available in the system before they can be borrowed.
  • Users must follow the assigned borrowing period.
  • Reference books and other restricted materials may only be used inside the library.

3. Book Return and Renewal Policy

  • Borrowed books must be returned on or before their assigned due dates.
  • Users may request a renewal if no other user has reserved the book.
  • The system must update the book's availability after a successful return.
  • Users must report lost or damaged books to the librarian.
  • Overdue books may result in borrowing restrictions, depending on library rules.

4. Book Reservation Policy

  • Users may reserve books that are currently borrowed by other users.
  • Reservations must be recorded in the system.
  • Users will be notified when their reserved books become available, if the notification feature is supported.
  • Reservations may expire if the user does not claim the book within the designated period.

5. Book Management Policy

  • Only authorized administrators or librarians may add, edit, or remove book records.
  • Each book record must contain essential information, such as its title, author, ISBN or book ID, category, and availability status.
  • Duplicate records should be avoided.
  • Book information must be updated whenever changes occur.
  • Books that are lost, damaged, or withdrawn must have their status updated accordingly.

6. User Conduct Policy

  • Users must handle library materials with care.
  • Users must not intentionally damage, alter, or remove library materials without authorization.
  • Users must respect other library users and maintain a suitable environment for reading and studying.
  • Misuse of the system may result in temporary account suspension or other appropriate action.

7. Data Privacy and Security Policy

  • The system must protect users' personal information and borrowing records.
  • Passwords must be stored securely using appropriate password hashing.
  • Access to administrative functions must be restricted to authorized personnel.
  • User information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.
  • The system must maintain backups to help prevent data loss.

8. System Maintenance Policy

  • The system must be maintained regularly to ensure proper functionality.
  • Database records should be backed up periodically.
  • Technical errors and security issues must be reported to the system administrator.
  • System updates should be tested before deployment.
  • Unauthorized modifications to the system or database are prohibited.

9. Penalty and Violation Policy

  • Overdue books may be subject to fines or borrowing restrictions, according to the library's approved rules.
  • Lost or damaged books may require replacement or compensation.
  • Repeated violations may result in temporary suspension of borrowing privileges.
  • All penalties must be applied consistently and communicated clearly to users.

10. Administrator Policy

  • The administrator is responsible for maintaining accurate book and user records.
  • The administrator may monitor borrowing, returning, and reservation transactions for legitimate library purposes.
  • Only authorized personnel may generate reports or modify important system settings.
  • Administrative activities should be recorded when audit logging is available.
